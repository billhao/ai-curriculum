# Lessons for curriculum guides

## 2026-11-04 — Teach the paper before evaluating it

The RSI catch-up guide mixed technical fragments, unexplained benchmark numbers,
limitations, and ranking judgments before establishing what each paper was about.
Appending another ranking made the learning route harder to follow.

For multi-paper guides, use the same visible structure for every main paper:
the problem, its contribution, a concrete explanation of the method, interpreted
evidence, and its significance and relevance to Hao's research. Connect new ideas
to his nanoGPT, SFT, and autonomous-research knowledge. Explain a result's task,
baseline, and meaning before giving caveats. Keep one current recommendation
order, justify each placement, and separate reading priority from claims about
scientific impact. Shorter optional entries still need a coherent explanation.

Before delivery, read each entry as a learner: can the reader explain what changed,
why it helps, what was demonstrated, and why to study it now?
